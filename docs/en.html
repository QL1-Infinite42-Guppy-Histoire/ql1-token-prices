How prices are calculated & caveats
  • The QOM price is tracked on the 42 Chain side: the QOM/42 pool ratio × the 42 price from the 42/USDT pool. It is also cross-checked through a second route (FISH/USDT × FISH/QOM) every time, to make sure the two are not far apart.
  • Each QL1 token's price uses, out of all 645 QomSwap pairs (fetched in full from the factory), the direct pair against an anchor token (WQOM / qUSDT / qUSDC / qDAI) with the deepest anchor-side liquidity. Pools with less than $0.5 of anchor-side liquidity are treated as "no liquidity" and get no price.
  • WQOM uses the QOM price above; qUSDT / qUSDC / qDAI are fixed at $1. When a token has two routes and the prices differ by more than 3×, it is flagged "⚠ Route divergence".
  • Market cap = price × total supply (FDV style). The total supply of bridged tokens (such as qUSDT) may include a mint cap, in which case the market cap reads larger than reality.
  • Prices for tokens with small pools are estimates. Always check the liquidity (anchor side) shown alongside. If liquidity is tiny compared to market cap, it does not mean that amount could actually be traded at the displayed price.
  • The underlying pool data comes from the on-chain QomSwap factory (the same pools Ave.ai displays). This site only changes the price "anchor" compared to Ave.ai.
